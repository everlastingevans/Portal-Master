# LaunchPath: Employer Marketing Scripts (LinkedIn & Facebook)

**October 2026 · For: employers, HR managers, founders and hiring managers in South Africa**

Use this pack for organic posts, paid ads, LinkedIn outreach and short video. Every claim here matches what the platform does today. Don't advertise the Hiring Partner subscription, bulk pricing tiers or the employer dashboard until those are switched on.

---

## 1. Core message

> **Tell us the role. We send you 3–5 screened candidates in five working days. You pay only when someone starts.**

**Positioning line (new, covers all levels):**
*"Screened shortlists for South African employers, from graduates to experienced professionals."*

**Who we're talking to:**
- **Founders and SME owners** who do their own hiring and have no time to read 300 CVs.
- **HR and talent managers** with too many open roles and too few people.
- **Hiring managers** (sales, marketing, ops, tech, finance) who keep interviewing people who don't fit.
- **Businesses with recruiter fatigue** who are tired of paying 15–25% agency fees and getting CV dumps.

## 2. Benefits for employers

### Managed recruitment (LaunchPath Hire)
1. **No upfront cost.** Submit a vacancy for free. No account, no retainer, no subscription.
2. **Pay only when your hire starts.** 7.5% of annual CTC, with a minimum of R7,500 and a **maximum of R18,000**. Most agencies charge 15–25% with no cap.
3. **60-day replacement guarantee.** If the hire doesn't work out early on, we search again.
4. **3–5 candidates, not 300 CVs.** You get a short list of people who've already been screened.
5. **Shortlist in five working days.** Our target for supported roles, counted from the calibration call.
6. **We learn what good looks like before we search.** A short calibration call covers must-haves, deal-breakers and team fit.
7. **Candidate Cards make comparison easy.** Each candidate shows experience, salary expectation, availability, key skills, a recruiter's note and screening results (match level, communication rating, role assessment score, interview readiness).
8. **Screening goes beyond the CV.** Candidates complete an AI-scored video interview, so you see how they communicate before you meet them.
9. **Fast, private review.** View your shortlist on a secure link with no login, and request interviews in one click.
10. **You see the fee up front.** The vacancy form shows a live fee estimate before you submit.

### Self-serve hiring tools (for teams that want to run their own hiring)
11. **AI writes your job ad.** Type a few details and get a complete, editable job description.
12. **Search the Talent Pool.** Filter candidates by skills, location, availability and interview score, then invite the best fits to apply.
13. **AI Recruiter Dossier.** One click gives you a fit score, key strengths, areas to probe and tailored interview questions for each applicant.
14. **WhatsApp outreach.** Send interview invites and updates on the channel candidates actually read.
15. **Team notes and ratings.** Your hiring team scores candidates on skills, communication and culture fit, and everyone sees the average.
16. **R1,999 per role.** One flat price per listing, paid through PayFast.

### Trust
17. **POPIA by design.** Candidate contact details stay private until the candidate applies.
18. **People make the decisions.** AI helps us screen, but a recruiter reviews every match and nobody is rejected automatically.

### Talent: graduates to experienced professionals
19. **One pool, every level.** Graduates and early-career talent, plus experienced professionals ready for their next move.
20. **Sales, Marketing, Business Operations, Technology and Finance**, across South Africa.

## 5. LinkedIn: direct outreach

Keep the messages short and personal, and don't pitch in the connection request. Only message people who are hiring now (they've posted a job or a "we're hiring" post).

**Connection request (≤300 characters)**
> Hi {First name}, I saw {Company} is hiring for {role}. I run LaunchPath, which helps SA teams fill roles like this. I'd be glad to connect.

**Message 1 (after they accept)**
> Thanks for connecting, {First name}.
>
> Quick one: is the {role} search going well? If you're getting lots of applicants but not many good ones, we can help. We send 3–5 screened candidates within five working days, and you pay only if one starts (capped at R18k, with a 60-day guarantee).
>
> If you'd like, send me the brief and I'll tell you honestly whether we can fill it.

**Follow-up (5–7 days later)**
> Hi {First name}, just checking in on the {role} search. If it's already filled, congratulations. If not, the form takes about five minutes and costs nothing: launchpath.co.za/find-candidates

**Stop after two follow-ups.**
